Article 4 of the EU AI Act has applied since 2 February 2025. It requires providers
and deployers of AI systems to take measures to ensure, to their best extent, a
sufficient level of AI literacy in their staff and other persons operating the systems
on their behalf. The measures account for their technical knowledge, experience,
education and training, and for the context the systems are used in.

The duty applies at any size, and it attaches to both parties: the provider that
builds the system and the deployer that runs it. The article leaves the form open.
Each organisation decides how it builds that competence, and has to be able to
demonstrate the result.

Compliance with Article 4 starts as a register. Per team: who uses which system, what
they were trained on, when, by whom, and how the result was checked. That register is
the first record a supervisory authority can ask for.

Paperwork follows practice.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 4.
